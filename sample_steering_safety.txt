# Electronic Power Steering — Sample System Description

Document ID: EPS-SAMPLE-001

## 1. System overview
The Electronic Power Steering (EPS) system assists the driver in steering the vehicle. It includes a steering torque sensor, an electronic control unit (ECU), an electric motor, and vehicle power supply connections.

## 2. Normal operation
The torque sensor measures steering input. The ECU processes sensor information and commands the electric motor to provide steering assistance.

## 3. Potential malfunction
An unintended motor torque command may cause unexpected steering assistance. A loss of steering assistance may increase the effort required from the driver.

## 4. Operational scenarios
The vehicle may be moving in urban traffic, travelling on a curved road, or manoeuvring at low speed.

## 5. Potential hazardous event
Unexpected steering assistance while travelling on a curved road could make vehicle control more difficult and may contribute to a collision.

## 6. Safety considerations
The system design should consider sensor plausibility checks, monitoring of ECU operation, detection of abnormal motor commands, and a defined response to detected faults.

## 7. Verification considerations
Potential verification evidence includes sensor fault tests, abnormal-command tests, fault-response timing measurements, and documented review of the safety requirements.
