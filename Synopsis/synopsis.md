# Synopsis
## Problem
HARA requires structured hazard identification, operational context, consistent S/E/C assessment, safety-goal drafting, and traceability.
## Solution
A local-first Streamlit assistant organizes HARA records, performs deterministic candidate ASIL lookup from a documented public secondary reference, drafts initial safety goals/FSR/TSR, retrieves evidence from uploaded documents, and exports traceability.
## Users
Students and engineers learning or prototyping functional-safety workflows. Formal decisions remain with qualified safety personnel.
## Limits
Educational prototype only; not certified and not a substitute for licensed ISO 26262 material.
